## ✨ Key Features

### 🗜️ Superior Compression
apex uses the latest Zstandard (zstd) compression algorithm, which is significantly faster and more efficient than traditional methods. Your files will be smaller, and the process will be quicker. No more waiting forever for large files to compress.

### 🧠 Smart Deduplication
Have multiple copies of the same file? apex is smart enough to recognize duplicates and store them only once. This means your backups take up much less space, saving you precious storage.

### 🔒 Military-Grade Encryption
Protect your sensitive information with strong encryption. apex allows you to password-protect your archives, ensuring that only you and those you trust can access the contents. Your privacy is our priority.

### 🛡️ Error Correction with Reed-Solomon
Imagine sending a package, and it gets damaged in transit. With apex, your data is protected with Reed-Solomon error correction. Even if parts of your archive get corrupted, apex can recover the original data. This is perfect for long-term storage and sharing over unreliable connections.

### ⚡ Multi-Threading for Speed
apex takes full advantage of your computer's processing power. By using multiple threads simultaneously, compression and decompression happen much faster. You'll spend less time waiting and more time doing.

### 📁 FastCDC Chunking
This advanced technique breaks down files into smart, variable-sized chunks. This allows for more efficient compression and deduplication, especially with large files that change slightly between versions.

### 🖥️ Command-Line Interface (CLI)
apex is operated through a simple command-line interface. Don't worry if you're not a programmer – the commands are straightforward, and this guide will walk you through everything you need to get started.

## 📖 How to Use apex – Simple Examples

Here are a few everyday examples to get you started. Open the apex application (the command-line window) and try these:

### Example 1: Compress a Folder
Type this command and press Enter:
```

```
This will create a compressed archive of your "my-documents" folder.

### Example 2: Compress with Password Protection

This creates an encrypted archive. You'll need to enter the password to extract the files later.
